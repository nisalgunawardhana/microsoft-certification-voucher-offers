## 🚀 How to Get It (Step by Step)

1. **Get the GitHub Student Developer Pack** — apply at [education.github.com/pack](https://education.github.com/pack) with your student status.
2. **Create a free DataCamp account** at [datacamp.com](https://www.datacamp.com).
3. **Claim 3 months of free DataCamp Premium** through the GitHub Student Developer Pack (DataCamp offer in the pack). The discount is only available to premium subscribers.
4. **Complete the full Data Analyst in Power BI career track** — [Data Analyst in Power BI](https://www.datacamp.com/tracks/data-analyst-in-power-bi) (17 courses, about 48 hours).
5. **Wait for the email from DataCamp** — Microsoft sends codes in batches and DataCamp distributes them. It can take **up to 3 days** after finishing the final course. If nothing arrives after 3 days, contact DataCamp Support.
6. **Redeem the 50% discount code** when you register for the exam (you have **3–6 months** to use it; the expiry date is in the email). See the [Microsoft Credentials page](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/?wt.mc_id=studentamb_365539).

> ⚠️ **Notes**
> - Finishing the track does **not** award the certification — only a 50% discount on the exam fee.
> - The code expires 3–6 months after it is issued, so schedule your exam soon after receiving it.
> - Offer terms are set by DataCamp and Microsoft and may change.

> 💡 With ~48 hours of content, plan your study time so you finish within your 3 free months.

## 📖 About the PL-300 Certification

PL-300 is an official, paid Microsoft certification. It measures your ability to prepare data, model data, visualize and analyze data, and deploy and maintain assets in Power BI.

## 🎯 Who Is It For?

People working as (or wanting to work as) a data analyst or business analyst. It's a strong way to show Power BI skills to employers.

## 🧠 How the Track Prepares You

The Data Analyst in Power BI track was developed in collaboration with Microsoft to help you pass PL-300. It teaches hands-on skills in Power Query, DAX, data modelling, and more.
